# Network Anatomy

UK electricity distribution cables, cut-outs and boards, taken apart.

**Open it:** https://jimmypop86.github.io/network-anatomy/

- 17 UK DNO cables (Waveform, Consac, PILC, concentric and split concentric services, triple concentric, LV ABC, 11 kV triplex, 3-core XLPE, PICAS and PILC, 33 kV), built layer by layer: strip them a layer at a time or look at the cut end in section.
- Cut-outs and boards taken apart in the real order, step by step with the tools: the 80/100 A house cut-out, three-phase sets, older black phenolic heads, the cast-iron biscuit tin, and a 12-way multi-service distribution board.
- Works on a PC (mouse and keyboard) and on a phone (drag to turn, pinch to zoom, tap a part).

For education only: always follow your own DNO's procedures and safety rules.

Built with Unity (Web).
